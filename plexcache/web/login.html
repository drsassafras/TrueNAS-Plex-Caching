Plex Cache

Sign in with the username and password from the app's settings in TrueNAS.

Finding or changing them: in TrueNAS, open Apps → Plex Cache → Edit. They're the PLEXCACHE_USERNAME and PLEXCACHE_PASSWORD lines under environment:. Change them and save; the app restarts and everyone is signed out.

The example password (change-me) is never accepted, since anyone could read it. Set your own before signing in.

Turning the login off: put a # in front of both lines (or remove them) and save. The web UI then opens without a password, for anyone on your network.
